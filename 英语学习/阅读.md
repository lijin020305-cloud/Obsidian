Many times, we are unhappy because we cannot live in the present. We carry too many things on our minds: we either worry about the future or regret the past. Many of our troubles are caused by thinking too much and doing too little. There is no such thing as a right choice in this world; it is only because we go all out that the choice we made becomes right. A person truly begins to age when they become resigned to their fate. My favorite attitude toward life is: "In the first half of your life, don't be afraid; in the second half, don't regret."

很多时候，我们不开心，是因为无法活在当下。我们心里装着太多事：要么焦虑未来，要么后悔过去。许多困扰，皆因想得太多、做得太少。这世上本没有正确的选择；只是因为我们全力以赴，当初的选择才变得正确。人真正的衰老，是从“认命”开始的。我最喜欢的人生态度是：“前半生不要怕，后半生不要悔。”
